Welcome to Rapid Telemetry

Analyze Formula 1 data with distance-based telemetry comparison.

1. Select: Use the top dropdowns to pick a Year, Event, and Session.
2. Load Driver: Select a driver to see their lap list. Click any lap to load it into Slot 1.
3. Compare: Switch to Slot 2 in the sidebar, select another driver, and pick a second lap.
4. Explore: Hover over charts to see the track position move, or use Pace Analysis for full race insights.
